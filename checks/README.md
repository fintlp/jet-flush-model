# Checks

Deterministic scripts behind the figures quoted in the model description. Run them from the repository root with Node.js 18 or later. Each script uses a fixed seed, so the numbers are the same on every run; the random designs are drawn uniformly over the simulator's slider ranges, as described in `lib.js`.

To compare with the released model, first get its code:

```bash
git fetch origin main
git show origin/main:model.js > /tmp/model-main.js
```

| Script | What it checks |
|---|---|
| `node checks/regression.js` | Every main result and the noise peak and energy of 200 random designs (seed 12345; jets, flush, bowl geometry, both flush buttons) agree with the stored 2.0 baseline `checks/baseline-2.0.json` to 1e-7 relative. Exits with code 1 on any difference. After a deliberate model change, write a new baseline with `--write` and say in the change log why the results moved. About 7 s. |
| `node checks/regression.js /tmp/model-main.js` | The same designs against another version of `model.js` (for example `git show origin/main:model.js`); any difference counts. Only the randomly drawn settings are passed on, so each version uses its own defaults and a changed coefficient shows up as well. Before 2.0, the full settings of one version were passed to both, which hid changed defaults. The wall film changed in 2.0, so against a 1.x model this fails by design. |
| `node checks/physics.js` | Physics checks with pass limits, 61 checks, about six minutes. A: energy and angular momentum of a frictionless film, water balance, physical ranges of all state variables, smoothness of peak swirl and of the main results under small design changes (large steps are refined 10 and 100 times and rerun at 2 ms, to tell jumps, steep slopes and time-step jitter apart). B: closed-form cases (cistern drain, steady pool level at constant inflow, level table, film on a vertical cylinder, paper removal). C: the pool level and pool swirl equations re-integrated with fourth-order Runge-Kutta at a 20 times finer step. D: checks proposed by the independent physics review (spatial convergence, siphon break, time step down to 1 ms, partial flush head, film front, monotone waste trends, round-bowl symmetry, rim line depth), that the noise calibration's evaluation equals the simulator's, and that the splash noise counts every entering film part at its own speed (D10, added after review finding R-01). A check tied to a documented open issue is reported as KNOWN and does not fail the run, except with `--strict`, which is meant for releases. In 2.0 all 61 pass, also with `--strict`. These checks show that the model solves its own equations correctly, not that the equations describe a real bowl. |
| `node checks/pinn.js` | The physics-informed network (pinn-core.js): from a flush simulated with a weir coefficient of 1.45, read 81 or 13 times with noise, the network and least squares both find it within 6 %; training is deterministic; with the record cut at 3 s the physics keeps the later level closer to the truth than the same network without physics. About 5 seconds. |
| `node checks/field.js` | The field surrogate (field-core.js): every test map's cleaned share equals the simulator's cleaning for that run (reference maps at the reference's cleaning dose, cell areas of the oval wall; added after review findings R-02 and R-03), learning curve of the data surrogate (80 to 88 % of the cleaned area matched with 80 runs, better than with 10), fast model alone above 93 %, the learned correction no worse than the fast model, deterministic. 6 checks, about 15 seconds. |
| `node checks/challenge.js` | The engineering challenge (challenge-core.js): recomputes the eight races the page shows and compares them with `challenge-data.js`; also the start misses the targets, race #1 (AI route fewer runs than the classic route), valid designs on slider steps, no repeated runs, determinism. About three minutes; `--quick` race #1 only; `--holdout` the hold-out set, races 9 to 16 (seeds 8 to 15), never shown on the page or used for tuning, with the same summary (median runs to target, totals, misses); `--write` regenerates `challenge-data.js` after a change to the model or the core. |
| `node checks/value.js` | What a faster design loop is worth (2.1, value-core.js): the medians, the per-project headline and the race-by-race savings recomputed from challenge-data.js; the calculator's arithmetic with the example values, a run cost and a build effort; empty and invalid input; every example value has a source; the figures written into why.html match the data and no setter is hidden in a comment; slides/figures.json holds the same figures. 17 checks, about a second. |
| `node checks/examples.js` | The example scripts (2.1.1): batch-csv.js runs, writes the promised columns, repeats exactly, and a row equals a direct model run; coef-sensitivity.js covers exactly the 13 model coefficients and prints them largest effect first; every range in both scripts equals the page's slider range. 8 checks, about 5 seconds. |
| `node checks/literature.js` | The presets against figures from published toilet studies, patents and test reports (sources page, sections 1 and 5), about 12 seconds. RANGE checks (peak and mean flush flow, trap diameter) are backed by more than one source and fail the run when outside. COMPARE lines (film turns on the wall, water held in the bowl, water at rest, start of outflow, wall shear, best trap diameter, siphon peak flow, peak flow over random designs) come from a single source or a figure defined differently from the model's; they give a verdict and a reason and never fail the run. This is a plausibility check, not a validation. |
| `node checks/resolution.js` | How much cleaning, rim line and noise depend on how often the jet films are retraced: simulator and coarse settings against retracing at every step, including the short, strong flush that was the worst case in 1.3 (section 11). Takes about two minutes. |
| `node checks/timestep.js` | How much results change when the time step is halved (section 11) |
| `node checks/presets.js` | Preset figures quoted in section 10: vortex dips, margins, air core and siphon break times, source shares, Gordon cross-check, siphonic against washdown |
| `node checks/noise-range.js` | Width of the sampled noise range, and interior coefficient values outside the corner range (section 10) |
| `node checks/timing.js /tmp/model-main.js` | Run time against the other model version: both presets, then 60 random designs at full resolution and in the coarse mode of the sweep and the search, with medians, 10 and 90 % points and the ratio of the totals. The ratio is what section 11 quotes (2.0 against 1.3.1: about 1.6 and 1.35); absolute times depend on the machine. About a minute. |
| `node checks/browser.js` | The simulator page in a headless browser: presets and trap type, the 25 mm rim gap in sliders and sweep, undo and Demo me history, pinned reference, AI lab details, the noise calibration (A-weighting of test tones, the demo fit, a generated WAV file, meter readings), the tolerance study, the robust optimiser search, the progress bar with Cancel and Stop, the engineering challenge with its live recomputation and guided tour, the CFD check, the physics-informed network, the field surrogate, the three-part story, the why page timeline, that Demo me finds a design meeting every target, keyboard and screen reader behaviour of tabs, charts and the story, and that the header with its why card, the explore page and its links, the tutorials page with its screenshots and quoted results, the why page figures and calculation boxes, the savings calculator and part 4 of the story, the development chain, roles and levels on the why page (complete, no company named, phone layout without sideways scrolling), and that the six pages load their fonts from the page itself and nothing from other hosts, and that a design handed over in the link is applied once without overwriting the visitor's saved design (105 checks, about ten minutes). Needs Playwright: `npm install --no-save playwright && npx playwright install chromium`. Exits with code 1 on any failure. |
