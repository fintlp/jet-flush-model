The calibration workflow from the model description, end to end. A hidden reference model plays the role of PreonLab: it uses different coefficients and adds effects the physics model cannot represent. The tool fits six coefficients to a few reference runs with Nelder-Mead, adds a Gaussian-process correction for what is left, and checks all three versions on designs it never saw.

The reference data is synthetic. With real PreonLab runs the steps are the same.

    How the model works

    1 · Cistern and jets

    The cistern drains through the flush valve and supply path into the rim nozzles. Jet speed follows the head above the nozzles, v = √(2gH / (1+K)), so flow falls as the cistern empties.

    2 · Wall film and cleaning

    Each jet is traced as a spreading film on the oval bowl wall. Gravity and turbulent wall friction act on it, and the wall holds it on its curved path, which gives the centrifugal and spin-up effects. Wall shear τ = ½ρcfu² is accumulated per cell over the flush.

    3 · Pool swirl

    Film arriving at the pool brings angular momentum. The pool is treated as a rotating body that loses momentum through wall friction and outflow. Its swirl speed sets the vortex dip and how well floating paper is drawn to the outlet.

    4 · Outflow and waste

    A washdown bowl spills over the trap weir. A siphonic bowl first fills its trapway, then primes and draws the pool down until air breaks the siphon. Waste leaves when trapway velocity exceeds a transport threshold, limited by a maximum solids concentration.

    Calibrating it

    Three to five reference runs fix the coefficients. After that the model interpolates between them at this speed. Fit in this order, because later groups depend on earlier ones.

    Known limits

    • The bowl is an idealised oval with the same axis ratio at every depth. Real bowls change shape with depth and have the trap inlet off-centre.
    • The film is quasi-steady, stays attached to the wall and does not splash or interact with the other jet.
    • The pool rotates as a solid body. A real vortex core is deeper than the dip shown.
    • Waste is lumped into two classes. Clogging, media shape and drain-line carry are not modelled.
    • Status chips use display bands chosen for this tool, not values from a standard.
